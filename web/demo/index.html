Drag to look around. W A S D to fly, R and F to rise and fall, the mouse wheel to move. Click a planet, or press the bracket keys, to focus it; Escape returns to free flight. Click a drone, a crew member in the ship's portholes, or a piece of debris (a stale branch), to open its panel. The files button opens the directory view: arrow keys move through it, Enter focuses a file or folder on the map, Escape closes it.

Commet

Your codebase as a pocket universe.

A pixel-art star system of a code repository. Agent-written code starts under cloud, and you chart it by reading the change. Fly around, open the planets, read a diff, and watch the fog clear.

DEMO: invented data

Every repository, person and agent session here is made up. The demo runs entirely in your browser: nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere.

The source, on GitHub. Made for Qodo's "Build a Software Factory in 30 Days".
