# Astronaut lab: variant parameters

Generated by `npm run lab` from `variants.js` and `params.js`. Each variant is a parameter set for the one astronaut in `astronaut.js`; mixing variants is mixing rows of this table. Lengths are in figure heights (the figure is always exactly 1 tall before the scene scale, 0.95), angles in radians. **Bold** marks a value that differs from A (the current spec).

## Leanings

- **A · Current spec:** The approved chibi as it ships: 57% helmet, one smooth bent tube per limb. The reference.
- **B · Soft vinyl toy:** Pudgier and rounder: egg torso, fat even limbs and very wide blends, so every joint melts in.
- **C · Hard-suit engineer:** Crisp: ring collars at shoulders and hips, accordion elbows and knees, cuffs, neck seal, boxy torso and pack.
- **D · Lanky cadet:** Taller chibi: 45% helmet on a neck seal, longer gently tapered limbs, slim torso and pack.
- **E · Pocket nub:** Ultra-chibi: 63% helmet over a little egg body, with tiny straight nubs for arms and legs.
- **F · Heroic taper:** A broad wedge chest, limbs thick at the root tapering to small mitts and boots.
- **G · Ball-joint doll:** Articulated figure: slender tubes between small gear-tone spheres at the shoulders, elbows and knees; hips stay blended.
- **H · Pear bean:** Bottom-heavy: pear torso, slightly squashed dome, short splayed legs in big cuffed boots.
- **I · Retro diver:** A wide squashed dome on a thick collar, boxy torso, chunky square pack, ring shoulders and cuffs.
- **J · Sleek racer:** Streamlined: egg torso on a neck seal, slim smoothly tapered limbs, thin cuffs, slim rounded pack.
